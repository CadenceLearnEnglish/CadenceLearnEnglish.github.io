How do I ask about renting an apartment in English?

Start with “Is the apartment still available?” Then ask “How much is the rent per month?” and “Are bills included?” To visit, say “Can I come for a viewing this week?” Before you sign, ask “How much is the deposit, and when do I get it back?” and “How long is the lease?” (Cadence Talk Episode 4) Go to Episode 4 · Short answer page

How do I say I use my phone too much in English?

Say “I spend too much time on my phone” or “I keep checking my phone.” Keep + verb-ing means you do it again and again. To change the habit, say “I’m trying to cut down on screen time,” and at night, “I’m going to put my phone away.” Cadence Talk Episode 8 teaches these phrases.

How do I politely ask someone to explain something in English?

Say “Could you explain this part, please?” It is polite, clear and works everywhere: with a landlord, a contract, a teacher or a colleague. Point to the part you mean, listen, then check you understood: “So, the deposit comes back when I leave?” Cadence Talk Episode 4 practises this phrase. Go to Episode 4

How do I ask if a business idea will work in English?

Ask “Is there a market for this?” It means: do enough people really want it, and will they pay for it? Use it for a product, a service or a side project, for example: “It’s a great idea. Is there a market for this in our city?” Cadence Talk Episode 6, Starting a Small Business, teaches it.

What does digital detox mean in English?

A digital detox is a short break from screens, like one evening or one day at the weekend. It does not mean you stop using your phone forever. You can invite friends: “Let’s have a phone-free dinner.” Or say: “I’m trying to cut down on screen time.” Cadence Talk Episode 8 explains it.

How do I answer “Tell me about yourself” in a job interview?

Answer in three parts: who you are now, what you have done, and why you want this job. Keep it under one minute. For example: “I’m a marketing graduate. I worked on social media for two years. Now I want to grow in a bigger team like yours.” Cadence Talk Episode 3, First Job Interview, shows how. Go to Episode 3 · Short answer page

What can I say in English when I don’t understand a question?

Say “Sorry, could you repeat that, please?” If you need time to think, say “That’s a good question. Let me think for a moment.” Both phrases sound calm and polite, and asking is always better than guessing. Cadence Talk Episode 3, First Job Interview in English, practises both phrases in a real interview. Go to Episode 3 · Short answer page

How do I stop being afraid to speak English?

Treat every mistake as proof that you are trying, and build a small speaking habit each day. Say one new sentence out loud, shadow a short line, or talk to yourself for two minutes. Confidence grows from practice, not from being perfect. Cadence Talk Episode 2, Afraid to Speak English?, is all about this. Go to Episode 2 · Short answer page

What English level is Cadence Talk for?

Cadence Talk is for learners at A2 to B1 level. Each episode is short, 3 to 6 minutes, with easy, natural English, 4 to 6 useful phrases and a quick review at the end. Start with Cadence Talk Episode 1, Never Give Up, then listen to a new episode every day on YouTube, Spotify or Apple Podcasts. Go to Episode 1
