Questions learners ask

Short answers first. Each one comes from a Cadence Talk episode.

How do I answer "Tell me about yourself" in a job interview?

Answer in three parts: who you are now, what you have done, and why you want this job. Keep it under one minute and do not tell your whole life story. Example: "I'm a marketing graduate. I worked on social media for a small company for two years. Now I want to grow in a bigger team like yours." (Episode 3)

What can I say when I don't understand a question?

Say "Sorry, could you repeat that, please?" If you need time to think, say "That's a good question. Let me think for a moment." It gives you time and sounds calm. Asking is better than guessing. (Episode 3)

What is shadowing in English?

Shadowing means listening to a short sentence and saying it out loud right after the speaker, copying the rhythm, stress and sounds. Practise a few minutes every day with Shadowing Time.

How do I stop being afraid to speak English?

Treat mistakes as proof that you are trying, and build small daily speaking habits. Episode 2 of Cadence Talk is all about this.

Is Cadence free?

Yes. Every episode and every shadowing video is free on YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

What level is Cadence Talk for?

Easy, natural English at A2 to B1 level. Each episode is about three minutes, with 4 to 6 useful phrases and a quick review at the end.
