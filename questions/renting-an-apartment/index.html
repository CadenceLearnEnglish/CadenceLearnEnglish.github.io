How do I ask about renting an apartment in English?

Short answer

Start with “Is the apartment still available?” Then ask “How much is the rent per month?” and “Are bills included?” To visit, say “Can I come for a viewing this week?” Before you sign, ask “How much is the deposit, and when do I get it back?” and “How long is the lease?” (Cadence Talk Episode 4)

Phrases to practise

  • “Is the apartment still available?”
  • “How much is the rent per month?”
  • “Are bills included?”
  • “Can I come for a viewing this week?”
  • “How much is the deposit, and when do I get it back?”
  • “How long is the lease?”

Say each phrase out loud two or three times, then try it in your own sentence.
